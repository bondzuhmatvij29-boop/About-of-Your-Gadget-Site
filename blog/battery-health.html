How to check your phone’s battery health on Android

Android does not show one universal “battery health” number on every phone. The information is in different places depending on the manufacturer and Android version.

Where to look

Open Settings, then search for “battery”. Many phones show a battery page with usage and charge information. Some Samsung, Xiaomi and Google Pixel models also show a health or condition entry there. If you do not find one, the battery information in the device’s own system app is the next place to check.

What the numbers mean

Charge level is the current percentage. Temperature that stays high while charging or idle can point to a problem. Health or condition, where shown, estimates how much of the original capacity remains. Lower capacity means the battery holds less charge and empties faster.

When to worry

Some battery wear is normal over years of use. Get a professional check if the phone overheats, shuts down at a high charge level, or drains very fast even when idle.
